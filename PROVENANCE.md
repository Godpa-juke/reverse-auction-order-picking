# Provenance

This public artifact is a source-only export of the reverse-auction portion of a larger human–robot warehouse simulator.

- Canonical source revision: `a9a7eec1666a67505646c0ca0f858e0a2e8754ad`
- Upstream base: [`semitable/robotic-warehouse`](https://github.com/semitable/robotic-warehouse)
- Upstream license: MIT; the original copyright and license are preserved in `LICENSE`.
- Export date: 2026-08-24

The public export keeps executable simulator and assignment code. It excludes private warehouse orders, facility-specific maps, precomputed path arrays, raw experiment outputs, manuscripts, and internal operational files. The included tests and synthetic example require none of those excluded assets.
